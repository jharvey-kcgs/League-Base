# Privacy Policy for League Base

**Last updated: [September 2026]**

This is a starting draft, not a document reviewed by a lawyer — accurate
to how the app actually works today, but worth a second look before
treating it as final, especially if the app's data handling ever
changes.

## The short version

League Base does not collect, store, or transmit any personal
information about you. There is no account, no login, and no server of
ours in the middle. The only data the app touches is:

- **Your favorite team and theme preference** — stored only on your own
  device, never sent anywhere
- **Public League of Legends esports data** (team rosters, match
  schedules, results, standings, VODs) — fetched directly from
  lolesports.com's own public API, the same data anyone can see on
  their website

## What we don't do

- No account creation or login of any kind
- No analytics, crash reporting, or usage tracking
- No advertising, and no ad networks embedded in the app
- No access to your contacts, photos, location, camera, or microphone
- No data is ever sold, rented, or shared with any third party for
  marketing purposes — there is no data collected to share in the
  first place

## What's stored on your device

Two things, and only two things, are saved locally:

1. **Your favorite team** — the team you pick during onboarding, used
   to theme the app around that team's colors. You can change this
   anytime in Settings.
2. **Your theme preference** — light mode, dark mode, or system default.

Neither of these ever leaves your device. There is no backend server
for this app to send them to, and no mechanism in the app that
transmits them anywhere.

## Data from lolesports.com

League Base displays live League of Legends esports information — team
names and logos, schedules, match results, standings, and video-on-demand
links. This data is fetched directly from lolesports.com's own public,
read-only API, the same publicly available data source anyone can view
by visiting lolesports.com in a browser.

These requests are one-directional: the app asks lolesports.com for
public sports data, and nothing about you (your identity, device, or
usage of the app) is included in or sent along with that request beyond
what's technically required for any standard internet connection to
function (like your device's IP address, which lolesports.com's own
servers would see the same way any website sees any visitor — this
isn't something League Base adds, controls, or has access to itself).

Team names, logos, colors, and roster information shown in the app are
also compiled from Liquipedia's publicly available pages.

## Children's privacy

League Base does not knowingly collect any personal information from
anyone, including children. Since the app has no account system, no
data collection, and no way for anyone to submit personal information
through it at all, there is nothing to collect regardless of the age
of who's using it.

## Changes to this policy

If how this app handles data ever changes — for example, if a future
version adds a feature that does collect or transmit information — this
policy will be updated to reflect that honestly, with a new "Last
updated" date at the top, before any such change ships.
